About Us

Wanderlust Travels & Tourism

Wanderlust Travels is a premier travel agency located in New Delhi, India. Established in 2014, we have been on a mission to make quality travel experiences accessible to every traveler regardless of their budget.

We offer curated domestic and international tour packages, honeymoon specials, adventure treks, and group tours. Our experienced travel consultants, strong hotel partnerships, and customer-first approach have helped 50,000+ travelers explore their dream destinations.

With IATA certification and recognition by the Ministry of Tourism, Government of India, our services are trusted by travelers across the country.

Whether you are a solo traveler, couple, or family group, Wanderlust Travels has the perfect package for you.
