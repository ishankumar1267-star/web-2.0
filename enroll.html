Frequently Asked Questions

How does the tour booking process work?
Submit this online reservation form. Our destination specialist will contact you with a detailed itinerary, hotel options, and price breakdown. Once you approve, you can pay a token deposit to confirm your booking.
Can I customize the itinerary and hotel categories?
Yes, absolutely! 100% of our packages can be tailored to your dates, pace, hotel choices, and specific sightseeing desires.
What payment methods do you accept?
We accept UPI (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm), Net Banking, Debit/Credit Cards, and direct bank transfers. You can also pay at our Delhi office.
What is your cancellation and refund policy?
We offer flexible cancellation policies. Depending on the destination and hotel terms, full or partial refunds are provided for cancellations made well ahead of the travel date.
